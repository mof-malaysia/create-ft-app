@AGENTS.md

The general rules for working on your own (what to do without asking, when to stop and ask, how to report a finished task) are in your global `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`. AGENTS.md adds this project's commands, checks and stop-and-ask items.

Work comes from GitHub issues labelled `ready`; anything that needs a product decision gets `needs-decision`. `ROADMAP.md` is owned by the product owner: propose changes to it, don't make them.
